# Referral Protocol

The goal is a clean, defensible handoff to one human. A referral changes who reads
the candidate; it does not erase a tenure gap or force a hiring manager to re-level
the role.

## 1. Canonicalize the Relationship First

Before researching or messaging through a new connector, add the person through
the canonical workflow that maintains the configured profile source, then re-read
the updated or regenerated source. Record enough provenance to avoid rediscovery:
name, relationship source, company or reach when known, date introduced, and
current status. If the owning workflow is unknown, stop and ask the user; never
hand-edit a generated profile export.

For existing connectors, verify current contact-attempt state elsewhere in the
pipeline. A connector roster entry alone is not proof of an unworked opportunity;
missing or stale contact evidence remains `unknown`.

## 2. Pick One Primary Ask

Choose the best-fit req using the posting body, job-routing doctrine, and current
pipeline state. If one person can route multiple companies or roles:

- make one concrete primary ask;
- state secondary options as secondary, not equal demands;
- never spend the referrer's credibility on a cold list dump.

## 3. Choose One Resume for This Human

Read `artifacts` from the validated profile source. Identify variants by their
rendered headline, never their shipment folder. Before a send, verify the selected
artifact path is a readable regular file. Send one resume per referrer human,
even when the person can route several roles. Per-role variants are only for
different recipient humans.

Headline matching beats body tailoring. Use verified existing inventory by
default. If a headline names a role the candidate never held, do not send it.
Repairing that headline is a defect fix, not a tailoring build, and is always
allowed: block the send, repair/rebuild it through the workflow that owns the
profile source, and re-verify the artifact before use. The no-new-build default
cannot preserve a lie.

## 4. Send the Complete Package Up Front

The initial referral send contains all three:

1. the chosen CV;
2. the exact req link;
3. a truthful years-gap disclosure derived from the profile source and posting.

Disclosure template:

> The one gap I see is that the role asks for **[required years]**, while I have
> **[profile-source-backed years]**. I wanted to put that up front so you can decide with
> full context.

Hebrew template:

> חוסר ההתאמה היחיד שאני רואה הוא דרישה ל־**[שנות ניסיון נדרשות]** מול
> **[שנות הניסיון לפי מקור הפרופיל]** שיש לי. חשוב לי להגיד את זה מראש כדי שתוכל/י
> להחליט עם התמונה המלאה.

Do not promise that the referral offsets the gap. The disclosure protects the
referrer and lets them decide whether to spend credibility.

## 5. Ask for a Cheap, Useful Outcome

Make one ask: route this req, or tell me quickly if the level is a hard no. Include
“feedback if it is not a fit” when the person is submitting to a real reviewer.

A fast disclosed no is a successful protocol outcome:

- the candidate avoids a long false-positive wait;
- the referrer was not surprised;
- the relationship stays warm;
- the human may redirect the candidate to a better-level door.

Do not interpret that no as evidence the disclosure failed.

## 6. Update Pipeline State

After the response, record submitted/rejected/no-reply/redirected state through
the canonical workflow that maintains the profile source, then regenerate and
re-read it when applicable. Before any direct application, check whether an
internal referral already created an ATS record; a duplicate cold apply can
damage referral source attribution.
