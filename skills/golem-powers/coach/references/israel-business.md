# Israeli Business Reference

> This reference contains general knowledge about Israeli business administration for freelancers. Tax rates and thresholds change annually — always brain_search for stored updates and flag data older than 6 months.

## Freelancer Types

### Osek Patur (Exempt Dealer)
- Below the annual revenue threshold (changes yearly — brain_search "osek patur threshold")
- Does NOT charge VAT
- Simpler bookkeeping
- Cannot deduct input VAT (VAT paid on business expenses)

### Osek Murshe (Authorized Dealer)
- Above the threshold, or voluntarily registered
- MUST charge VAT on all invoices
- CAN deduct input VAT
- Bi-monthly VAT reporting to mas hachnasa (tax authority)
- More complex bookkeeping but better for higher-revenue freelancers

### When to Switch
- Approaching the threshold: register as Murshe proactively (penalties for late registration)
- High business expenses: Murshe lets you deduct VAT, which can be significant
- Working with businesses: some prefer Murshe vendors (they can deduct your VAT)

## Bituach Leumi (National Insurance)

- Mandatory for all self-employed
- Payments based on income brackets
- Covers: health insurance, disability, old age pension
- Advance payments required (estimated quarterly)
- Annual reconciliation after tax filing

## Tax Reporting

- Annual tax return (doch shnati) — due by April 30 (extensions available)
- Bi-monthly VAT report (if Osek Murshe)
- Keep all receipts (digital OK)
- Business expenses: home office deduction, equipment, software, internet, phone

## Invoicing Requirements

Every invoice (heshbonit mas) must include:
1. Your full name and business number
2. Client's name and business number (if business)
3. Sequential invoice number
4. Date
5. Description of service
6. Amount before VAT
7. VAT amount (if Osek Murshe)
8. Total amount

### Invoice vs. Receipt
- **Heshbonit Mas** (tax invoice) — issued when billing
- **Kabala** (receipt) — issued when payment received
- **Heshbonit Mas / Kabala** — combined document (common for immediate payment)

## Banking for Freelancers

- Separate business account recommended (not required for Osek Patur)
- Keep clear records of business vs. personal transactions
- Business credit card simplifies expense tracking

## Important Contacts

- **Mas Hachnasa (Tax Authority):** For tax questions and filings
- **Bituach Leumi:** For insurance payments and claims
- **Accountant:** Recommended for annual filing, required for Osek Murshe

## Disclaimer

This is general reference information, not legal or tax advice. Israeli tax law changes frequently. For specific decisions (registration type, deductions, structuring), consult a licensed accountant (ro'e heshbon) or tax advisor (yo'etz mas).
